Microsoft PowerToys Keyboard Manager
------------------------------------

Simpilot vendors and compiles portions of Microsoft PowerToys Keyboard
Manager. The following files are reused unchanged or as an unchanged extracted
definition:

  third_party/PowerToys/KeyboardManager/LowlevelKeyboardEvent.h
  third_party/PowerToys/KeyboardManager/ModifierKey.h
  third_party/PowerToys/KeyboardManager/KeyboardHookDecision.h

The following files are minimal dependency-removal adaptations of PowerToys
Shortcut and KeyboardManagerState recording code. WinUI, XAML, chord-remapping,
layout-map, logging and PowerToys runtime dependencies are omitted:

  third_party/PowerToys/KeyboardManager/Shortcut.h
  third_party/PowerToys/KeyboardManager/Shortcut.cpp
  third_party/PowerToys/KeyboardManager/KeyboardManagerState.h
  third_party/PowerToys/KeyboardManager/KeyboardManagerState.cpp

The recording branch and hook-decision structure in src/app/keyboard_manager.cpp
follow KeyboardManagerEditor/KeyboardManagerEditor.cpp. Detailed provenance and the
adaptation boundary are documented in docs/powertoys-keyboard-recorder.md.
The vendored license is also available at third_party/PowerToys/LICENSE.txt.
